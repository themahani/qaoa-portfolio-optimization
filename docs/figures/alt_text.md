**qaoa_vs_classical.png**

Two panels. Left, grouped bar chart of the share of QAOA shots that are valid five-asset portfolios, by circuit depth: depth 1, 34% on a noiseless local simulation and 16% on IonQ's Forte 1 simulator; depth 2, 20% on a noiseless local simulation and 5% on IonQ's Forte 1 simulator; depth 3, 3% on a noiseless local simulation and 2% on IonQ's Forte 1 simulator. Random bitstrings would give 1.5%. Right, dot plot of the gap between each method's best valid portfolio and the exact optimum: Rounding the continuous solution reaches the optimum; Greedy selection (91 evaluations) reaches the optimum; Rounding + swap search (77 evaluations) reaches the optimum; 4,096 random valid portfolios is 0.9% worse; QAOA, depth 2 is 1.1% worse locally and 2.2% worse on IonQ's simulator; QAOA, depth 1 is 2.2% worse locally and 4.4% worse on IonQ's simulator; QAOA, depth 3 is 8.3% worse locally and 9.0% worse on IonQ's simulator.
